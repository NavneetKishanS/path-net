## Display and interpretation checks for P3

Label `ev_scn2a_r853q_lof_limitation` and `ev_scn2a_autism_lof_limitation` as **Scope limitation**, separately from the supporting finding. The shared contract has stance at edge level only; do not silently inherit a supporting edge's stance for every evidence row. If the sidecar is not available to the app, retain the limitations visibly in the edge note.
- Display `scn2a_single_gof_assignment_refuted` as **Contradictory evidence about a broad claim**. Exclude it from positive mechanism traversal and inference. Verification does not reverse its stance.
- Show R853Q as primarily LoF in the stated HEK assay, with the Xenopus gating-pore caveat. Show SCN2A subgroup membership as a cohort association, rather than a variant-function diagnosis.
- Keep broader-source/narrower-MONDO scope visible for community assets and research discovery links. STARR is an observational resource; KCNQ2 samples and study designs are candidates for discussion, not automatically reusable infrastructure for every linked disorder.
- Provisional cluster membership organizes records. Shared genes, symptoms, investigators or an ion-channel functional class do not establish common treatment, clinical compatibility, or a 10x acceleration. Those would require additional expert review and evidence.
