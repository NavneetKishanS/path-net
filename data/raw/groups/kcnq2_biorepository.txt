Different types of biological materials have been preserved and stored so that they can be requested by academic and industry professionals who are interested in studying KCNQ2-related disorders. What samples do we have? Thanks to your efforts, we have collected samples from 26 individuals with KCNQ2-related disorders, as well as from 13 people who do not have KCNQ2-related disorders (often used as control samples for comparison). With these samples, we have over 20 different genetic variants represented, and ages ranging from 1.5 - 14 years old. What can be done with these types of materials? Our current biorepository contains urine, plasma, whole blood, and dried blood spot cards. Each donated sample contains valuable biological information that helps researchers better understand how the body is functioning. Whole blood gives the most detailed genetic information, making it a great option for in-depth analyses. Dried blood spot cards capture less of this detail but are easy to collect and send, making them another great option. Urine and plasma samples provide additional information and can be used to analyze proteins and other small molecules in the body.
